Questions

Before you come

What is a dúa?

Our speciality: two discs of crisp pastry with a filling in between. The lid has a hole in the middle and is caramelised. There are sweet ones (crème brûlée, tiramisuki, lemon, matcha, chocolate, dulce de leche) and savoury ones (salmon or hummus).

When are you open?

Wednesday to Friday 8am–1pm and 4pm–8pm. Saturday and Sunday 9am–8pm. Closed Monday and Tuesday.

Can I take them away?

Yes. Dúas and mini dúas come in a box, perfect for a birthday or a treat at home.

Do you have sugar-free options?

Yes, the 70% chocolate ganache dúa with plant-based cream has no added sugar.

Where exactly are you?

Calle de Bravo Murillo 92 (Chamberí, 28003), about two minutes on foot from Cuatro Caminos metro (L1, L2, L6).

Can I order for a group?

Send us a WhatsApp with the day and how many of you there are, and we will tell you what we can prepare.
